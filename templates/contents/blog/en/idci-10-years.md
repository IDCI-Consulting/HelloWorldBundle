### Alain Mille

<span class="text-presentation">
<span class="image-presentation">
![Alain Mille](/images/blog/alain_mille.jpg "Alain Mille")
</span>
After a first carrier as an ingineer in medical applications of computer science at the Hospices Civils de Lyon (1974-1983), **Alain Mille** as developed the IT department of CPE Lyon (1984-1999).  
He defends a thesis on the reusability of the experience (water distribution), and defend his professorial thesis in 1998 and is hire as a professor at the University Lyon 1 in 2000.  
He contributes to the creation of the [LIRIS CNRS](https://liris.cnrs.fr/?set_language=fr) 5205 laboratory, creates the IT Ressources Complex of Lyon 1, sets the formal education of computer science in bachelor's degree and the courses in the field of artificial intelligence in master's degree.  
He leads industrials relations of the [LIRIS CNRS](https://liris.cnrs.fr/?set_language=fr) laboratoy and carries the responsibility of the graduate school of Computer science and Mathematics for four years.  
Researcher in artificial intelligence, he takes an interest in the development of the theory of the modeled trace of interactions.    

This research field has been developed in many industrial and scientific projects and is the subject of open software contributions.  
Twenty eight students defended their thesis under his direction, his publications are
available on [his LIRIS page](http://liris.cnrs.fr/membres?idn=amille).  
He presides over many lectures in the domains of artificial intelligence and web.  
Actually he is interested in developing [the commons of scientific knowledge](http://coexiscience.fr) and his involved in the association [Academics Without Borders](http://www.univ-sf.org ).  
</span>

___

During his lecture, **Alain Mille** will tackle first the question of the ethics in the digital age and then he will give an answer to the question: How to create the conditions for the emergence of a shared ethic between the actors of techno-scientific processes?
